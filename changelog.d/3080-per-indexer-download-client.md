### Added
- **Per-indexer download client** (#3080): each indexer now has an optional **Download client** setting in Settings → Indexers. Leave it on Default and nothing changes. Pick a client and every grab from that indexer goes to it: scheduled searches, the search page, held releases in Pending and Retry download. It overrides the client's priority and category routing, so a private tracker's torrents can live on their own qBittorrent. If that client is disabled, missing, the wrong protocol or not answering, the grab fails with a message naming the indexer (and the client, for an admin) and is never sent to another client. A scheduled search skips only that indexer's releases and still grabs from your others, and a held release from that indexer stays in Pending until its client is back. A download client that an indexer is pinned to cannot be deleted (the request is refused with the indexer names), and the indexer row shows a warning while its client is disabled, missing or the wrong protocol. Prowlarr syncs keep the setting.
