### Fixed
- **Covers Bindery stores itself show on the Wanted and Series pages, and a cover that fails to load shows "No cover"** (#3146). The Wanted list and the series endpoints returned stored covers (such as a Calibre library's cover) without routing them through the image proxy, the only way they load, so those rows showed a broken image. The endpoints that answer a change did the same, so saving a book, excluding it, deleting its file, enriching it or adding an author swapped its cover for "No cover" until the page was reloaded. They all hand back the proxied cover now. Every cover in the web app now also falls back to the same placeholder as a book with no cover when its image fails to load, whatever the reason, instead of the browser's broken-image icon.
