### Added
- **Bibliothèque nationale de France metadata provider** (#3144). The BnF can be chosen as the primary metadata provider in Settings, Metadata Profiles. Legal deposit puts nearly every book published in France in its catalogue under the French edition title, so a French library whose indexers name releases after the French edition ("Alice au pays des merveilles") gets that title instead of the original English one other providers list. Books carry their language, publisher, year, ISBNs and page count, and the original title of a translation; translators and illustrators are never taken for the author. It reads only BnF's printed book records, under the Licence Ouverte 2.0, credited in Settings and, with the date each record was retrieved, on every BnF book and author page, and fetches no covers. It is opt in: an install that does not select it never contacts the BnF, and when selected it sends at most one request a second. Thanks to mnivoliez for the request.
