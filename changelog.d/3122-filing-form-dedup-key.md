### Fixed
- **A title filed with its article last is the same book as the natural spelling** (#3122). A library row named `Trace of Death, A` (the way Calibre files sort titles) and a catalogue entry named `A Trace of Death` produced two different duplicate keys, so a catalogue sync created a second row for a book you already had. `The Hobbit` and `Hobbit, The`, `Der Prozess` and `Prozess, Der`, and the same shape for the other articles Review duplicates already knows now key as one title, and the sync and the importers match the row you have. Only a lone article after the last comma counts, so `Love, Actually` and `Me, Myself and I` are left alone. Existing rows are recomputed on the first start after the upgrade. Rows that would now share a key are not merged or removed: they stay as they are, and they show up together in Review duplicates for you to decide.
