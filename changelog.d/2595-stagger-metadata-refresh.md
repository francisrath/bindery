### Changed
- **Author profile refresh is spread over the day** (#2595). Instead of refreshing every author in one nightly burst, Bindery now refreshes a few authors an hour, the stalest first, and remembers when each one was last refreshed. Authors refreshed within the last 24 hours are skipped, an author whose refresh fails or is rate limited is retried on a later pass, and a Hardcover rate limit or daily quota hold pauses the work and resumes it on the next pass. The daily request total is unchanged (about one Hardcover request per author per day), but it no longer arrives all at once. A manual author Refresh still runs immediately and counts as that author's refresh for the day. See the request estimates for Free and Supporter allowances in the deployment guide.
